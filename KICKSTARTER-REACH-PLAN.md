# Crypteds Kickstarter Reach Plan

This document gives Josh a practical, measurable plan for expanding the
Crypteds Kickstarter audience without spending heavily on broad, unqualified
reach.

## Current result

The first Facebook boost reached approximately 800 people and produced 21
additional Kickstarter followers.

```text
21 followers / 800 people reached = 2.6% reach-to-follow rate
```

That result is not automatically poor. Reach includes people who saw the post
but never clicked it. A 2.6% conversion from impressions to Kickstarter
followers may be encouraging.

The promotion cannot be evaluated accurately without recording:

- Total amount spent
- Reach
- Frequency
- Link clicks
- Landing-page views
- Kickstarter followers added during the campaign
- Cost per link click
- Cost per landing-page view
- Estimated cost per Kickstarter follower

If 800 people saw the promotion, 21 followers may be a useful result. If 800
people clicked the link, the Kickstarter page, audience, or message needs
substantial improvement.

## Primary recommendation

Do not increase the budget on the existing boosted post yet.

Use Meta Ads Manager for the next experiment:

- Objective: `Traffic`
- Destination: Crypteds Kickstarter pre-launch page
- Optimization: `Landing-page views`, when available
- Call to action: `Follow on Kickstarter`
- Tracking: a separate Kickstarter referral or tagged link for every ad

Meta recommends the Traffic objective when sending people to an external or
third-party destination where the final action is difficult to measure through
Meta.

Official reference:
<https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/ad-objectives/traffic>

## Creative test

Test three different reasons to care. Keep the audience and budget consistent
so the creative is the main variable.

### Ad A: Character hook

Example:

> Which little horror would you let out of the crate?

Show several characters quickly and end with the Kickstarter follow action.

### Ad B: Crate reveal

Start with the closed crate. Reveal the character and costume within the first
two seconds.

### Ad C: Maker process

Show a fast progression:

1. Digital model or sculpt
2. Print or fabrication
3. Paint
4. Costume
5. Finished Crypted

### Production guidance

- Prefer vertical video for social feeds.
- Keep the first tests approximately 8 to 15 seconds.
- Show the product or reveal within the first two seconds.
- Use readable on-screen copy without requiring audio.
- End with one clear action: follow the project on Kickstarter.
- Do not ask viewers to like, comment, share, visit, and follow all at once.

## Audience strategy

Avoid treating all horror fans as one audience. Many horror-film fans will
enjoy an image without purchasing a physical collectible.

Test audiences that overlap with the actual product:

- Designer and art-toy collectors
- Horror collectible buyers
- Miniature painters
- 3D-printing and prop-making audiences
- Creature-design followers
- Cosplay builders
- Cute-but-creepy art communities
- Kickstarter collectible and tabletop backers
- Existing Furious Facades social engagers

Start narrow enough to learn which audience responds. Expand only after an
audience produces qualified landing-page visits and Kickstarter followers.

## Warm-audience outreach

People who already know Josh or Furious Facades are more likely to act than a
broad cold audience.

Reach out through permitted channels to:

- Existing Instagram and Facebook followers
- Past Etsy customers where platform rules permit contact
- Past commission customers
- Friends and collaborators who will genuinely share
- Local maker, cosplay, horror, art, and collectible communities
- People who previously engaged with Crypteds content

Use one specific request:

> Follow the Kickstarter now so you are notified when Crypteds launches.

Avoid vague requests such as “check this out.”

## Creator and community partnerships

Prioritize relevant small creators over one large generic influencer.

Potential partners:

- Horror-toy reviewers
- Designer-toy collectors
- Miniature painters
- Prop-making channels
- Cute-horror artists
- Creature-design accounts
- Kickstarter preview channels

Prepare a small outreach package:

- Three strong product images
- One short reveal video
- One behind-the-scenes clip
- A concise project description
- The Kickstarter follow link
- A unique tracking link for that partner
- A physical preview piece when practical

Personalize every request. Explain why Crypteds fits that creator's audience.
Do not send a generic bulk message.

## Kickstarter pre-launch activity

Use pre-launch updates to keep existing followers interested:

- Meet the first Crypted
- How a Crypted is made
- What comes inside the crate
- Reward preview
- Character vote or choice
- Launch-date announcement

Kickstarter sends notifications for pre-launch updates and now supports
follower-focused messages. Use updates around meaningful milestones instead of
posting so frequently that followers tune them out.

Official references:

- <https://help.kickstarter.com/hc/en-us/articles/360034769114-Setting-up-your-project-s-pre-launch-page>
- <https://start.kickstarter.com/kickstarter-release-notes/updates-to-project-followers>

## Follower target

Kickstarter reports that pre-launch followers are more likely to pledge than
people who discover a project after launch. Its published average is
approximately 32% of pre-launch followers pledging after launch.

Use this only as a planning estimate:

```text
Required early backers / 0.32 = target pre-launch followers
```

Examples:

| Desired early backers | Approximate follower target |
| ---: | ---: |
| 50 | 157 |
| 100 | 313 |
| 200 | 625 |

Actual results will depend on reward price, shipping, audience quality,
presentation, trust, and launch execution.

Official reference:
<https://updates.kickstarter.com/the-anatomy-of-a-great-kickstarter-prelaunch-page/>

## Recommended first paid experiment

Use a total test budget of approximately $30 to $60:

1. Create the three short creative variations.
2. Give each variation the same small budget.
3. Use the same audience for all three.
4. Optimize for landing-page views.
5. Give each ad its own tracking link.
6. Run long enough to collect a useful comparison.
7. Stop clearly weak creative.
8. Produce a second version of the strongest concept.

The goal is not maximum reach. The goal is discovering which combination of
creative and audience produces Kickstarter followers at a sustainable cost.

## Decision rules

- Do not judge success by likes, comments, impressions, or reach alone.
- Do not scale a campaign until it produces qualified visits and followers.
- Change one major variable at a time.
- Preserve the winning audience while testing new creative.
- Preserve the winning creative while testing new audiences.
- Prefer a small engaged community over a large indifferent audience.
- Keep claims factual. Do not invent scarcity, reviews, sales, or launch dates.
